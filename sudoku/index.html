How to play

Use 1, 2, 3 and 4 once in each row, column and little box.

  1. Tap an empty square, then choose a number below.
  2. Each number belongs only once in each row, column and little box. The heavier edges mark the boxes.
  3. The starting numbers stay fixed. Your numbers can be changed.

Easy: the next empty square is selected for you. Numbers that do not fit are greyed out.

Made a mistake? Select your number and tap the eraser, or use Undo to reverse your last move.
